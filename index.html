Features

Everything a sari-sari store actually needs

Built for the everyday tindahan — fast to learn, works without Wi-Fi, and keeps your listahan in order.

📦

Inventory tracking

Add products, set tingi or wholesale prices, and get low-stock alerts before your best-seller runs out.

📒

Utang ledger

Log utang per suki with dates and partial payments. See exactly who still owes and how much — no more guessing.

🧮

Daily sales compute

Record each sale in seconds and the script totals your kita at the end of the day — cash, utang, and change included.

🧾

Print resibo

Generate a clean resibo for any sale or utang payment. Works with your existing thermal printer or plain paper.

🔌

Runs fully offline

All data stays on your tindahan PC or phone. No account, no cloud, no signal needed — even during brownouts.

💾

Backup & restore

One-click backup to a USB or folder. Move your whole store history to a new device without retyping anything.

How it works

From download to first resibo in minutes

No installation wizard marathon. Set up your store, add your products, and start selling.

1

Download & open the script

Grab the latest release from GitHub, unzip it on your tindahan PC or phone, and launch the app. No internet required after setup.

2

Add products & suki

Enter your usual items — softdrinks, noodles, load, tingi — with prices and stock. Add customers you allow to utang.

3

Sell, log, and print resibo

Record each sale, log utang payments, and let the script compute your daily sales. Print a resibo whenever the customer asks.
